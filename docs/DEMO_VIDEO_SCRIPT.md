# Khata Saathi: demo video, 14 shots

Record the screen silently first, all fourteen clips. Record the voiceover afterwards and lay it on top. Total runs about 4 minutes 30. A cut at the end brings it to 4 minutes 10.

---

## Before you press record

1. **More → press and hold the "More" title → Reset demo data → Reset.**
2. **More → "I speak, Saathi replies in" → English.** Tamil comes only in shot 13.
3. Three Chrome tabs, left open on these pages:
   - **Tab 1** `http://localhost:8000` the shop app
   - **Tab 2** `http://localhost:8000/customer?id=1` Lakshmi's WhatsApp
   - **Tab 3** `http://localhost:8000/paytm?id=4` Ravi's Paytm app
4. Home should read **₹2,450 pending · 8 customers**.
5. Quiet room. You must speak into the mic in shot 2 even though that audio is discarded.
6. Record each shot as its own clip. Hold two seconds on every screen after it changes.

# Part 1 · Recording script

## Shot 1 · Home · 15 sec
Start on the home screen. Scroll slowly down past the mic, the quick actions, the Today card and both charts. Scroll back to the top.

## Shot 2 · Speak the entry · 30 sec
Tap the big mic. Say clearly: **"Give Lakshmi 150 rupees credit for 2 kg rice"**. Tap the mic again to stop. Hold two seconds on the read-back card, then tap **Save**.
*If it mishears the amount, do not stop. Tap the mic and say "No, 150".*

## Shot 3 · Quick actions · 25 sec
On the home screen, tap **Add by hand**. The sheet opens titled "Add udhaar by hand". Show the customer field and the kind chips, close with ✕.
Tap **Record payment**. The same sheet opens already set to **Cash received**. Close it.
Tap **Join my khata**. The QR dialog opens. Hold three seconds, then close.

## Shot 4 · Reminders · 15 sec
Tap the **Reminders** tab. Scroll to the bottom to Lakshmi's new row. Pause on the channel marks down the list. Tap the small **WhatsApp icon** on her row.

## Shot 5 · Customer confirms · 18 sec
Tab 2, Lakshmi's phone. The entry has arrived with two buttons. Hold a moment, then tap **Correct**.

## Shot 6 · Guardrails and remind · 24 sec
Back to Tab 1, Reminders. Tap the **ⓘ** icon, hold two seconds on the rules, tap to close.
Tap the **pen** on Lakshmi's row, show the message sheet with the WhatsApp preview, close with ✕.
Tap **Remind now**.

## Shot 7 · Customer pays · 23 sec
Tab 2. The reminder has arrived with a **Pay now** row. Tap it. The payment page opens. Tap **Pay ₹150**. Hold on the green tick.

## Shot 8 · Reconcile and the bell · 20 sec
Back to Tab 1. The toast announces the payment and Lakshmi has left the queue. Tap the **bell**. Show **Money in** with "Lakshmi paid ₹150 · settled up". Tap the **Reminders** filter chip.

## Shot 9 · The Paytm channel · 18 sec
**Reminders** tab. On **Ravi's** row tap the **wallet icon**. Tab 3 opens with the shop's reminder as a Paytm notification. Tap **Pay ₹290** to reveal the payment sheet with wallet, cards, net banking and UPI. Hold three seconds, close with ✕.

## Shot 10 · Amount wrong · 12 sec
Still on Tab 3. Tap **Amount wrong?**. The reply appears and the reminders switch turns off.

## Shot 11 · The task · 13 sec
Back to Tab 1. Tap the **bell**, now carrying a red badge. Hold on the **Needs you** card: "Ravi says the amount is wrong", with the Resolve button.

## Shot 12 · The khata · 17 sec
**Khata** tab. Tap **Settled**, then **Pending** again. Open **Ramesh (Gandhi Street)**. Show the amount, the four action tiles and the history. Tap back.

## Shot 13 · More and what's next · 30 sec
**More** tab. Scroll to the **Coming soon** card and hold on the three rows: Entries from the Soundbox, Voice activation phrase, Family khata.
Tap the language row and choose **தமிழ்**. Visit **Saathi**, then **Khata**, then **Reminders**, so Tamil shows on three screens.

## Shot 14 · Close · 10 sec
Back to the home screen. Hold still on the mic.

# Part 2 · Voiceover script

Record after the picture is cut. Steady pace, about 165 words a minute. One breath between shots.

**1 · Home**
> This is Khata Saathi, a voice-first collection assistant for Paytm merchants. One screen. A greeting, a microphone, and the shop's money at a glance. Today's cash, who owes what, and this week's udhaar against collections.

**2 · Speak the entry**
> A customer takes goods on credit. The shopkeeper does not type, he speaks. Saathi hears the name, the amount and the items, and reads it back before saving anything. That is our rule. The AI understands, the code calculates. Nothing reaches the ledger until the merchant says yes.

**3 · Quick actions**
> Voice is the fastest way, but never the only way. The same entry can be typed by hand, and a payment taken at the counter is recorded the same way. And this QR code sits by the till. A customer scans it once, and from then on their entries and reminders reach them on WhatsApp, with their consent.

**4 · Reminders**
> The entry is in the shop's ledger now, and Lakshmi appears in the reminder queue. Each row shows how long the money has been pending, and which channel that customer uses. Lakshmi is on WhatsApp.

**5 · Customer confirms**
> And this is Lakshmi's phone. The entry has already arrived, in her language, with two buttons. Correct, or wrong amount. Every entry is two-sided. She confirms it. That one step ends the oldest argument in every kirana shop. I never took that much.

**6 · Guardrails and remind**
> Back in the shop app. Reminders are polite by design. Only with consent, at most one a day, nine in the morning to eight at night, paused if an entry is disputed, and stopped the moment the balance is zero. The merchant can read the exact message and add a line of his own. Now he sends it.

**7 · Customer pays**
> Lakshmi gets the reminder with a Paytm link, and pays in one tap, in full or in part. The payment is verified, so the balance updates on its own and the reminders stop. Only a verified payment closes an udhaar.

**8 · Reconcile and the bell**
> Back on the shop's screen the balance is already zero, and Saathi announces it out loud. Everything that happened sits under the bell. Money in, reminders sent, customers who joined.

**9 · The Paytm channel**
> Not every customer uses WhatsApp. Ravi gets the same reminder inside the Paytm app, as a notification. He pays with wallet, UPI, cards or net banking, without leaving Paytm.

**10 · Amount wrong**
> And if he thinks the amount is wrong, he says so right there. Reminders pause at once.

**11 · The task**
> The shopkeeper gets a task, not a silent failure. The bell separates what needs him now from what simply happened. Nobody is chased while a dispute is open.

**12 · The khata**
> The khata itself. Pending first, settled behind a tap, and search across every customer. Open anyone and you see the real record. No score, no black box. The shopkeeper decides.

**13 · More and what's next**
> Next, the entry will not even need a phone. The shopkeeper speaks to the Paytm Soundbox already sitting on the counter, or says "Hey Saathi" hands-free, and a family shares one khata. And the whole app speaks the shopkeeper's language. Tamil, Kannada, Hindi, Telugu or English. Every screen, every reminder, every notification. A kirana owner never has to read English to run his own khata.

**14 · Close**
> Built on Sarvam speech and the Paytm ecosystem, for one point two crore kirana shops. Khata Saathi. Record udhaar. Track every rupee. Collect with dignity.

## If it runs long

Cut **shot 12**, the khata tour, and its paragraph. It is the least surprising stretch, and the Khata tab is visible behind several other shots. That brings the film to about 4 minutes 10.

## While recording

- Hold two seconds after every screen change. Fast cursors look nervous.
- One clip per shot. Re-recording one shot costs 20 seconds.
- If speech recognition stumbles twice in shot 2, tap the mic and tap a **Try saying** bubble instead. It looks the same on screen.
- Keep the cursor off the phone frame while a screen is being read.
- Cut the picture first, then trim clips to fit your voice, never the other way round.

## Do not show

- The hidden demo tools, the clock, or Reset.
- The legacy one-screen UI at /legacy.
- The More tab beyond Coming soon and the language row.
