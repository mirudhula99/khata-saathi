# Khata Saathi: demo video, 14 shots, about 5 minutes

Record the picture silently first, all fourteen clips. Record the voiceover afterwards and lay it on top.
Shots total about 4 minutes 55. The voiceover is 636 words, about 3 minutes 50 of speech, which leaves air between shots.

---

## Before you press record

1. **More → press and hold the "More" title → Reset demo data → Reset.**
2. **More → "I speak, Saathi replies in" → English.** Tamil comes only in shot 14.
3. Three Chrome tabs, left open:
   - **Tab 1** `http://localhost:8000` the shop app
   - **Tab 2** `http://localhost:8000/customer?id=1` Lakshmi's WhatsApp
   - **Tab 3** `http://localhost:8000/paytm?id=4` Ravi's Paytm app
4. Home should read **₹2,450 pending · 8 customers**.
5. One clip per shot. Hold two seconds after every screen change.

# Part 1 · Recording

**1 · Home · 15s**
Scroll slowly down past the mic, the quick actions, the Today card and both charts. Scroll back to the top.

**2 · Speak the entry · 30s**
Tap the mic. Say **"Give Lakshmi 150 rupees credit for 2 kg rice"**. Tap to stop. Hold two seconds on the read-back, tap **Save**. If it mishears, tap the mic and say **"No, 150"**.

**3 · Quick actions · 25s**
Tap **Add by hand**, show the sheet, close. Tap **Record payment**, note it opens on **Cash received**, close. Tap **Join my khata**, hold three seconds on the QR, close.

**4 · Reminders · 15s**
Reminders tab. Scroll to Lakshmi at the bottom. Pause on the channel marks down the list. Tap her **WhatsApp icon**.

**5 · Customer confirms · 18s**
Tab 2. The entry has arrived with two buttons. Tap **Correct**.

**6 · The message and the send · 22s**
Back to Tab 1, Reminders. Tap the **pen** on Lakshmi's row. Show the WhatsApp preview and the line the merchant can add. Close with ✕. Tap **Remind now**.

**7 · Customer pays · 20s**
Tab 2. Tap **Pay now**, then **Pay ₹150**. Hold on the green tick.

**8 · It lands in the shop app · 12s**
Back to Tab 1. The toast announces the payment, Lakshmi leaves the reminder queue, and the charts move.

**9 · The customer page · 35s**
Still in Reminders, tap a customer's **name row**, for example Ramesh. His page opens. Hold on the amount and the days, then on the four tiles: **Remind now, Record payment, Send statement, Customer's phone**. Scroll through the **history**. Keep scrolling to the WhatsApp and reminders card: the **consent switch**, **Reminders via WhatsApp or Paytm app**, and the **language** row.

**10 · Khata, pending, and a payment · 25s**
**Khata** tab. The **Pending** chip is already selected. Tap **Priya**. On her page tap **Record payment**. Choose **Cash**, leave the amount at ₹180, tap **Note cash**. Saathi reads it back. Tap **Save**. She settles.

**11 · The bell · 15s**
Tap the **bell** in the top bar. Hold on **Priya paid ₹180 · settled up** under Money in. Tap the **All** chip to show reminders sent and customers who joined.

**12 · More, and editing a customer · 18s**
**More** tab. Scroll to the customer list, tap **Ramesh (Teacher)**. Hold on **Unique label**, then on **Remove customer**, then close with ✕.

**13 · Coming soon · 20s**
Still in More, scroll to **Coming soon**. Hold on each row: **Entries from the Soundbox**, **Voice activation phrase**, **Family khata**.

**14 · Language and close · 25s**
Tap the language row and choose **தமிழ்**. Visit **Khata**, then **Reminders**, then back to **Saathi**. Hold still on the mic.

# Part 2 · Voiceover

Steady pace, about 165 words a minute. One breath between shots.

**1 · Home**
> This is Khata Saathi, a voice-first collection assistant for Paytm merchants. One screen. A greeting, a microphone, and the shop's money at a glance. Today's cash, who owes what, and this week's udhaar against collections.

**2 · Speak the entry**
> A customer takes goods on credit. The shopkeeper does not type, he speaks. Saathi hears the name, the amount and the items, and reads it back before anything is saved. That is our rule. The AI understands, the code calculates. Nothing reaches the ledger until the merchant says yes.

**3 · Quick actions**
> Voice is the fastest way, but never the only way. The same entry can be typed by hand, and a payment taken at the counter is recorded the same way. And this QR code sits by the till. A customer scans it once, and from then on their entries and reminders reach them on WhatsApp, with their consent.

**4 · Reminders**
> The entry is in the ledger now, and Lakshmi joins the reminder queue. Every row shows how long the money has been pending, and which channel that customer uses. Lakshmi is on WhatsApp.

**5 · Customer confirms**
> This is Lakshmi's phone. The entry has already arrived, in her language, with two buttons. Correct, or wrong amount. Every entry is two-sided. She confirms it, and that single step ends the oldest argument in every kirana shop. I never took that much.

**6 · The message and the send**
> Before sending, the shopkeeper can read the exact message the customer will get, and add a line of his own. Reminders are polite by design. Only with consent, at most one a day, nine in the morning to eight at night, and they stop the moment the balance is zero.

**7 · Customer pays**
> Lakshmi gets the reminder with a Paytm link and pays in one tap, in full or in part. The payment is verified, so the balance updates on its own and the reminders stop. Only a verified payment closes an udhaar.

**8 · It lands in the shop app**
> Back in the shop, Saathi announces it out loud and the khata updates itself. The shopkeeper never has to tick anything off.

**9 · The customer page**
> Tap any customer and you get the whole relationship on one page. What they owe, and for how long. Remind them now, record a payment, or send a full statement to their phone. Below that, every entry and every repayment, exactly as it happened. And this is where the shopkeeper chooses how that customer hears from the shop. WhatsApp, or inside the Paytm app. Their language. Their consent, which only they can take back.

**10 · Khata, pending, and a payment**
> The khata itself opens on the people who matter today. Pending first, settled behind a tap. Priya has come to pay. Record payment, cash at the counter, and Saathi reads it back before it is saved, exactly like a spoken entry.

**11 · The bell**
> Everything that happened sits under the bell. Priya has paid in full and is settled up. Money in, reminders sent, customers who joined. And anything that needs the shopkeeper now sits on top.

**12 · More, and editing a customer**
> In More, every customer can be edited. Name, mobile number, what they owe, and a unique label. That label matters. Two customers called Ramesh, one is the teacher, one is from Gandhi Street, and the money never lands on the wrong man. A customer with no history can be removed.

**13 · Coming soon**
> Next, the entry will not even need a phone. The shopkeeper speaks to the Paytm Soundbox already sitting on the counter. Says "Hey Saathi" and starts hands-free. And a whole family shares one khata, so a son can pay what his mother owes.

**14 · Language and close**
> And the whole app speaks the shopkeeper's language. Tamil, Kannada, Hindi, Telugu or English. Every screen, every reminder, every notification follows. A kirana owner never has to read English to run his own khata. Khata Saathi. Record udhaar. Track every rupee. Collect with dignity.

## Notes

- **Priya pays ₹180, not ₹380.** The ledger refuses any repayment larger than the balance, so an extra ₹200 cannot be recorded. Her full ₹180 settles her, and the bell then reads "Priya paid ₹180 · settled up".
- **Ravi's Paytm app is not in this cut.** The Paytm channel is covered on the customer page in shot 9, through the "Reminders via" row. If you want the Paytm screen itself, add a 20-second shot after shot 9: Reminders, tap the **wallet icon** on Ravi's row, show the notification and the payment sheet, then tap **Amount wrong?**.
- **If it runs long**, cut shot 12. That saves 18 seconds and loses the least.

## While recording

- Hold two seconds after every screen change. Fast cursors look nervous.
- One clip per shot, so a fumble costs 20 seconds, not five minutes.
- If speech recognition stumbles twice in shot 2, tap the mic and tap a **Try saying** bubble. It looks the same on screen.
- Keep the cursor off the phone frame while a screen is being read.
- Cut the picture first, then trim clips to fit your voice.

## Do not show

- The hidden demo tools, the clock, or Reset.
- The legacy one-screen UI at /legacy.
