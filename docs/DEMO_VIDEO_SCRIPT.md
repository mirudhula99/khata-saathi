# Khata Saathi: demo video script

Your flow: speak an entry, customer confirms on WhatsApp, remind, customer pays, reconcile, notifications, the Paytm channel, then Khata and More. About 3 minutes 30 seconds. A cut list at the end gets you to 2 minutes 45.

Record the screen with no microphone track, then record your voiceover separately and lay it on top.

---

## Before you press record

1. Server running. **More → press and hold the "More" title → Reset demo data → Reset.**
2. **More → "I speak, Saathi replies in" → English.** You switch to Tamil only in the last shot.
3. Open three Chrome tabs and leave them on these pages:
   - **Tab 1** `http://localhost:8000` the shop app
   - **Tab 2** `http://localhost:8000/customer?id=1` Lakshmi's WhatsApp
   - **Tab 3** `http://localhost:8000/paytm?id=4` Ravi's Paytm app
4. Home charts should read **₹2,450 pending · 8 customers**.
5. Quiet room. You must actually speak into the mic even though that audio is thrown away.
6. One thing to expect: after you save Lakshmi's entry she is **last in the reminder queue**, because the list is sorted by days pending and hers is new. Scroll to the bottom to find her.

## Shot 1 · Home · 0:00 to 0:15

**DO:** Start on the home screen. Scroll slowly down past the mic, the quick actions, the Today card and the two charts, then scroll back to the top.

**SAY:**
> This is Khata Saathi, a voice-first collection assistant for Paytm merchants. One screen. A greeting, a microphone, and the shop's money at a glance. Today's cash, who owes what, and this week's udhaar against collections.

---

## Shot 2 · Speak the entry · 0:15 to 0:45

**DO:** Tap the big mic. The listening screen opens with the waveform. Say clearly:

> Give Lakshmi 150 rupees credit for 2 kg rice

Tap the mic again to stop. The read-back card appears. Hold two seconds, then tap **Save**.

**SAY:**
> A customer takes goods on credit. The shopkeeper does not type, he speaks. Saathi hears the name, the amount and the items, and reads it back before saving anything. That is our rule. The AI understands, the code calculates. Nothing reaches the ledger until the merchant says yes.

---

## Shot 3 · Reminders · 0:45 to 1:00

**DO:** Tap the **Reminders** tab. Scroll to the bottom of the queue to Lakshmi's row. Hover over the channel marks so they are visible. Tap the small **WhatsApp icon** on her row.

**SAY:**
> The entry is in the shop's ledger now, and Lakshmi appears in the reminder queue. Each row shows how long the money has been pending, and which channel that customer uses. Lakshmi is on WhatsApp.

---

## Shot 4 · Customer confirms · 1:00 to 1:18

**DO:** Tab 2 is Lakshmi's phone. The entry has arrived with two buttons. Hold a moment, then tap **Correct**.

**SAY:**
> And this is Lakshmi's phone. The entry has already arrived, in her language, with two buttons. Correct, or wrong amount. Every entry is two-sided. She confirms it. That one step ends the oldest argument in every kirana shop. I never took that much.

---

## Shot 5 · Guardrails and remind · 1:18 to 1:42

**DO:** Back to tab 1, Reminders. Tap the **ⓘ** icon, let the rules bubble show for two seconds, tap to close. Tap the **pen** on Lakshmi's row, show the message sheet with the WhatsApp preview, close it with ✕. Now tap **Remind now** on her row.

**SAY:**
> Back in the shop app. Reminders are polite by design. Only with consent, at most one a day, nine in the morning to eight at night, paused if an entry is disputed, and stopped the moment the balance is zero. The merchant can read the exact message and add a line of his own. Now he sends it.

---

## Shot 6 · Customer pays · 1:42 to 2:05

**DO:** Switch to tab 2. The reminder has arrived with a **Pay now** row. Tap it. The Paytm payment page opens. Tap **Pay ₹150**. The green tick appears.

**SAY:**
> Lakshmi gets the reminder with a Paytm link, and pays in one tap, in full or in part. The payment is verified, so the balance updates on its own and the reminders stop. Only a verified payment closes an udhaar. Cash is recorded separately.

---

## Shot 7 · Reconcile and the bell · 2:05 to 2:25

**DO:** Back to tab 1. The toast announces the payment and Lakshmi is gone from the queue. Tap the **bell** in the top bar. Show **Money in** with "Lakshmi paid ₹150 · settled up", then tap the **Reminders** filter chip.

**SAY:**
> Back on the shop's screen the balance is already zero, the reminder has stopped, and Saathi announces it out loud. Everything that happened sits under the bell. Money in, reminders sent, customers who joined.

---

## Shot 8 · The Paytm channel · 2:25 to 2:50

**DO:** Reminders tab. On **Ravi's** row tap the small **wallet icon**. Tab 3 opens, his Paytm app, with the shop's reminder as a notification. Tap **Pay ₹290** to show the Paytm payment sheet with wallet, cards, net banking and UPI, then close it with ✕. Now tap **Amount wrong?**.

**SAY:**
> Not every customer uses WhatsApp. Ravi gets the same reminder inside the Paytm app, as a notification, and can pay with wallet, UPI, cards or net banking. And if he thinks the amount is wrong, he says so right there. Reminders pause at once.

---

## Shot 9 · The task · 2:50 to 3:03

**DO:** Back to tab 1. Tap the **bell**. The red badge is up. Show the **Needs you** card with "Ravi says the amount is wrong", and the Resolve button.

**SAY:**
> The shopkeeper gets a task, not a silent failure. The bell separates what needs him now from what simply happened. Nobody is chased while a dispute is open.

---

## Shot 10 · The khata · 3:03 to 3:20

**DO:** **Khata** tab. Tap **Settled**, then **Pending** again. Tap **Ramesh (Gandhi Street)** to open his page. Show the amount, the four action tiles and his history, then tap back.

**SAY:**
> The khata itself. Pending first, settled behind a tap, and search across every customer. Open anyone and you see the real record. No score, no black box. The shopkeeper decides.

---

## Shot 11 · Language · 3:20 to 3:40

**DO:** **More** tab. Tap the language row and choose **தமிழ்**. Go to **Saathi**, then **Khata**, then **Reminders**, so Tamil is visible on three different screens.

**SAY:**
> And the whole app speaks the shopkeeper's language. Tamil, Kannada, Hindi, Telugu or English. Every screen, every reminder, every notification. A kirana owner never has to read English to run his own khata.

---

## Shot 12 · Close · 3:40 to 3:50

**DO:** Back to the home screen. Hold still on the mic.

**SAY:**
> Built on Sarvam speech and the Paytm ecosystem, for one point two crore kirana shops. Khata Saathi. Record udhaar. Track every rupee. Collect with dignity.

## To cut it to 2 minutes 45

Drop **shot 10** entirely, and shorten **shot 11** to the language switch plus one screen. Remove these two paragraphs from the voiceover:

- "The khata itself. Pending first, settled behind a tap…"
- "…Every screen, every reminder, every notification." Keep the first and last sentence of that paragraph.

---

## While recording

- Hold two seconds on every screen after it changes. Fast cursors look nervous on video.
- Record each shot separately and join them. Re-recording one shot costs 20 seconds; re-recording the whole thing costs 4 minutes.
- If speech recognition stumbles twice on shot 2, tap the mic and tap a **Try saying** bubble instead. On screen it looks the same.
- If it hears the wrong amount, do not stop. Tap the mic and say **"No, 150"**. The correction is a feature and it looks good on video.
- Keep the cursor off the phone frame while a screen is being read.
- Edit the picture first, then stretch or trim shots to fit your voice, never the other way round.

## Do not show

- The hidden demo tools, the clock, or Reset. They explain the stage, not the product.
- The legacy one-screen UI at /legacy.
- The More tab beyond the language row.
